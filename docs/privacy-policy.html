Oh Pi App 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 8 月 10 日 · English below

Oh Pi App 是一个连接你自建 ohpi-gateway 的聊天客户端。本政策说明我们如何处理你的信息。核心事实：开发者不收集、不存储、不共享你的任何个人信息或对话内容。

1. 我们收集什么

开发者不收集任何数据。Oh Pi App 没有账号系统，没有广告，没有统计分析 SDK，不使用 IDFA 或任何跟踪技术，也不会向开发者或第三方发送分析、广告、跟踪或 App 使用数据。只有当你主动连接自建服务器或选择打开网站时，相关数据才会发送至你指定的服务器或网站。

2. 你的数据在哪里

  • 对话内容：只存储在你自行配置和拥有的服务器上（你部署的 ohpi-gateway 及 pi 工作区）。App 与你的服务器直接通信，数据不经过开发者。
  • 服务器配置（网关地址、访问令牌、SSH 主机/凭据）：只保存在你的设备本地，用于连接你自己的服务器，不上传、不共享。
  • 本地缓存：App 会在设备私有目录缓存部分会话数据以支持离线恢复，仅存于本机。

3. 数据传输

App 直接连接你配置的服务器（局域网或公网）。使用 SSH 隧道模式时，整条链路由 SSH 加密。局域网直连模式下，请仅在可信网络中使用，并妥善保管你的访问令牌——令牌泄露等同于服务器被访问。

4. 网页与外部链接

在 iOS 上，App 仅将 localhost、回环 IP 地址以及 .localhost 域名的 HTTP/HTTPS 主页面显示在内置网页视图中；其他 HTTP/HTTPS 链接会交给系统默认浏览器打开。网页内容及其发起的网络请求由相应服务控制，并受该服务及浏览器的隐私政策约束。App 不记录你的浏览历史，也不会将外部链接的访问情况发送给开发者。

5. 你的控制权

  • 随时可以在设置中删除服务器配置。
  • 删除会话会永久删除服务器端会话目录及其中的对话数据。
  • 卸载 App 即清除全部本地缓存与配置。

6. 儿童隐私

本 App 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们也不收集任何年龄段的个人信息。

7. 政策更新

如本政策发生变化，将在此页面更新并更新生效日期。重大变化会通过新版本说明告知。

8. 联系我们

如有隐私相关问题，请发送邮件至：yearsyan@hotmail.com

Oh Pi App Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 10, 2026

Oh Pi App is a chat client that connects to ohpi-gateway instances you host yourself. This policy explains how we handle your information. The key fact: the developer does not collect, store, or share any personal information or conversation content.

1. What we collect

The developer collects no data. Oh Pi App has no account system, no ads, no analytics SDKs, and no IDFA or tracking technology. It sends no analytics, advertising, tracking, or app-usage data to the developer or third parties. Data is sent only when you intentionally connect to a self-hosted server or choose to open a website, and then only to the server or website you selected.

2. Where your data lives

  • Conversations: stored only on servers you configure and own (your self-hosted ohpi-gateway and pi workspace). The app talks directly to your server; data never passes through the developer.
  • Server configuration (gateway address, access token, SSH host/credentials): stored only on your device to connect to your own servers. Never uploaded or shared.
  • Local cache: the app may cache conversation data in its private on-device directory to support offline recovery. Device-local only.

3. Data transmission

The app connects directly to the server you configure (LAN or public internet). In SSH tunnel mode, the entire connection is encrypted by SSH. In direct LAN mode, only use trusted networks and guard your access token — a leaked token is equivalent to access to your server.

4. Web pages and external links

On iOS, the app displays HTTP/HTTPS top-level pages in its embedded web view only when they use localhost, a loopback IP address, or a .localhost domain. Other HTTP/HTTPS links open in the system's default browser. Web content and any network requests it initiates are controlled by the relevant service and are subject to that service's and the browser's privacy policies. The app does not record your browsing history or send information about external-link visits to the developer.

5. Your control

  • Delete server configurations anytime from Settings.
  • Deleting a session permanently removes its data from your server.
  • Uninstalling the app erases all local cache and configuration.

6. Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not collect personal information from anyone.

7. Policy changes

If this policy changes, it will be updated on this page with a new effective date. Material changes will also be noted in release notes.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, email: yearsyan@hotmail.com
